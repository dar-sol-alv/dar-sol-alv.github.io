## Overview
Final rendition of the individual block diagram that connects to the rest of Team 205's boards. This board focuses on the lighting the system and the implementation of the Overide On/Off button and a reset. The device is planned to be powered by the 5V coming from the central hub board but has the capabilities to be powered by a barrel jack input. This section recieves the already deciphered readings from the motion and sound detectors and had the light behave accordingly. When it recieves the trigger and checks what the current settings for the lights are and activates for a period of time and at a certan brightness. The overide button effectively leaves the light on until the overide button is presssed again, to which the light goes back to responding to triggers from visual and audio cues.
